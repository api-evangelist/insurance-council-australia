# Insurance Council of Australia

> The Insurance Council of Australia (ICA) is the representative body for Australia's general insurance industry; its members write approximately 90% of total premium income for private sector general insurers and reinsurers. It administers the General Insurance Code of Practice, declares catastrophes and significant events, runs a member Data Hub, and advocates on resilience, climate risk and insurance affordability. It is a market body, not a carrier: it sells no policies and exposes no quote, bind, issue or FNOL API.

## What exists

- ICA publishes **no developer portal and no documented API**. developer/developers/docs/api/apis/data/datahub subdomains do not resolve; /developers/, /developer/, /partners/ and /integrations/ return 404.
- **Watch the homonym trap**: https://insurancecouncil.com.au/api/ returns 200 but redirects to /insurer/apia/ — the member profile for the Australian insurer brand Apia (Australian Pensioners Insurance Agency). It is not an API.
- The only integration surface is the **member portal login wall** (Azure AD B2C OpenID Connect) for member insurers.
- ICA's flagship data asset — normalised historical catastrophe losses back to 1967 — is a downloadable **XLSX spreadsheet**, not an API.
- Two incidental machine-readable surfaces respond anonymously: the standard **WordPress REST API** at /wp-json/ and the standard **RSS feed** at /feed/. Neither is documented by ICA and neither carries an insurance data model.

## APIs

- [WordPress REST API (incidental)](https://insurancecouncil.com.au/wp-json/wp/v2): anonymous read access to pages, media (the PDF publication corpus), categories, tags, taxonomies, statuses, comments and site search. 420 routes across 28 namespaces. /wp/v2/posts is empty and /wp/v2/users returns 401. The insurer, resource, ins-type, ins-topic and campaign custom post types are NOT registered with the REST API.
- [News RSS feed](https://insurancecouncil.com.au/feed/): RSS 2.0, application/rss+xml. The only first-party syndication surface.
- [MCP adapter (gated)](https://insurancecouncil.com.au/wp-json/mcp/mcp-adapter-default-server): a WordPress MCP server route is registered — namespace discovery returns 200 — but JSON-RPC initialize and tools/list return 401 rest_forbidden. Not publicly usable.
